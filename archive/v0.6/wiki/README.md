# Wiki

Short runbooks distilled from the [playbook](https://github.com/seintun/claude-guides/blob/guide-v0.6/playbook/agent-harness-playbook.md). Each one gives the steps, a checklist, the templates to copy, the pitfalls, and links into the playbook for the detail. A wiki page adds no facts of its own; when it and the playbook disagree, the playbook wins.

|---|---|---|

| [Grill](https://github.com/seintun/claude-guides/blob/guide-v0.6/wiki/grill.md) | Settling the design with one question at a time before any plan or spec; includes how to validate and test a grill | 1 Shape |
| [Orchestrate](https://github.com/seintun/claude-guides/blob/guide-v0.6/wiki/orchestrate.md) | Deciding what the lead does and what goes to helper agents | 4 Orchestrate, 5 Build |
| [Verify](https://github.com/seintun/claude-guides/blob/guide-v0.6/wiki/verify.md) | Turning "done when" into gates, receipts and independent review | 6 Check |

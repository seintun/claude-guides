# /prompt variants

Skill versions that were tested but not shipped, kept so their battle-test rounds can be reproduced. The shipped skill is `skills/prompt/SKILL.md`.

| File | Version | Why it was not shipped |
|---|---|---|
| [SKILL-v5-checker-trial.md](SKILL-v5-checker-trial.md) | The checker trial, a v5 variant: a fresh read-only checker runs the consistency pass (at most 2 rounds), drafts aim under about 100 lines, review rules only for multi-round reviews | Round 9: five times fewer contradictions than v5, but blind reviewers preferred v5 in 11 of 12 pairs because the shorter drafts dropped checks the task needed. See [results.md](../results.md) |
| [SKILL-v8.md](SKILL-v8.md), [delegation-v8.md](delegation-v8.md), [example-v8.md](example-v8.md), [check_prompt-v8.py](check_prompt-v8.py) | v8: a risk line sets which parts a prompt needs, delegation rules load on demand, GOAL comes first, a 20-line example, and the playbook rules v7 lacked ([docs/plans/prompt-v8.md](../../../../docs/plans/prompt-v8.md)) | Round 12 and dry-run round 6: it scored higher on the playbook (66% against 55%) and the external sources (91% against 75%), but blind reviewers preferred v7 in 11 of 12 pairs, its drafts lost a must-have in 5 of 6 scenarios, and it failed 7 of 60 dry-run checks against 2 for v7. Shorter drafts dropped checks again, as in round 9 |
| [SKILL-v9.md](SKILL-v9.md), [example-v9.md](example-v9.md), [check_prompt-v9.py](check_prompt-v9.py) (with v8's [delegation-v8.md](delegation-v8.md)) | v9: v8's playbook-backed gains with Templates B and L's sizes (`trivial`, `normal`, `high`), Template B's trivial form, a decisive-check rule, length set by risk, and three rules aimed at v8's dry-run failures ([docs/plans/prompt-v9.md](../../../../docs/plans/prompt-v9.md)) | Round 13: blind reviewers preferred v7 in 7 of 12 pairs (7.7 against 7.2), feature fell 2.0 points and bugfix 1.5, the research draft lost its decision rule (must-have R2), the audit found 12 contradictions against 8, and dry-run round 7 gave 13 failures in 60 against 6. The must-haves probe on feature and migration had passed. Drafts were 10% shorter than v7's |
| [SKILL-v10-D3.md](SKILL-v10-D3.md) | v10's bundle (R1 to R6) plus D3: each DONE WHEN check names the likeliest way the result could be faked or fall short and fails on it, and the step-10 reviewer asks which check could pass while the GOAL is unmet ([docs/plans/prompt-v10.md](../../../../docs/plans/prompt-v10.md)) | Round 14: it passed the bugfix and refactor probe (7.75 against 7.25) and lost no must-have, but over all six scenarios it scored 7.58 against the bundle's 7.42. A gain of 0.17 is inside the 0.25 noise margin, and D3 had to beat the bundle by more than the margin to ship |
